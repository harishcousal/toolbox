# Toolbox Spell Checker

Welcome to Toolbox Spell Checker! Follow the instructions below to check spelling, correct mistakes, and work with text files and spreadsheets.

## How to Use

### 1. Check Spelling

1. Open the Spell Checker.
2. Type your text into the **Your text** field or paste text from another source.
3. Click **Check spelling** to identify potential spelling mistakes.
4. Misspelled words will be highlighted in the text.
5. Click a highlighted word to view correction suggestions.

### 2. Apply Corrections

1. Click a highlighted word to display the available suggestions.
2. Select the correct word to replace the misspelling.
3. Use **Ignore** if the word is correct and you don't want to change it.
4. Use **Add to dictionary** to save a valid word to your personal dictionary.
5. Repeat until you've reviewed the text.

### 3. Choose Your Dictionary

Select your preferred language from the dictionary menu:

- **English (US)** — American English spelling.
- **English (UK)** — British English spelling.

Choose the appropriate dictionary before checking your text.

### 4. Review Your Corrections

Use the **Before & after** section to review your changes.

- **Compare:** View the original and corrected text together.
- **Original:** View only the original text.
- **Corrected:** View only the corrected text.

Corrected words are highlighted in green in the corrected preview, while the original text highlights the corresponding words in red.

### 5. Upload a Text File

1. Click **Upload TXT**.
2. Select a `.txt` file from your computer.
3. Review the loaded text and check its spelling.
4. Apply corrections as needed.
5. Use the available download option to save your results.

### 6. Check an Excel or CSV File

1. Click the Excel/CSV upload option.
2. Select the spreadsheet you want to check.
3. Choose the worksheet, if applicable.
4. Select the column containing the text you want to check.
5. Click **Load selected column into Your text**.
6. Review the imported text and correct spelling mistakes.
7. Click **Save corrected text to Excel** to place the corrected values in a separate column.
8. Click **Download corrected Excel** to download the updated file.
9. Open the downloaded file and verify the corrected values.

**Important:** The original column is preserved. Review the downloaded spreadsheet before using it in your work.

### 7. Clear the Current Work

Use **Clear text** to clear the text editor.

Use **Clear Excel** to remove the currently loaded spreadsheet and reset the Excel workflow.

Make sure you have downloaded any results you want to keep before clearing your work.

## Important Notes

- This tool focuses on spelling, not grammar or punctuation correction.
- Review suggestions carefully, especially for technical terms, equipment descriptions, part numbers, and model numbers.
- A suggested correction may not always be the word you intended.
- Personal dictionary entries are saved in your current browser. They are not automatically synchronized across devices.
- Keep a backup of important spreadsheets before making corrections.
- After downloading a corrected spreadsheet, verify that the corrected values match the appropriate original rows.
